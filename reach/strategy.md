# Spring Branch Littles: 90-day growth strategy

Written 2026-10-25 (launch week). Review every Tuesday; change only when our data says so.

## Positioning

**One Monday email that tells Spring Branch & Memorial parents what's on for little kids over the next two weeks (storytimes, free events, kids-eat-free nights), put together by a local mom.**
What we're not: a blog, an ad circular, or a citywide list where half the events are an hour away.

**How the first 250 add up (the launch math):**
- Owner's own network (texts, group chats, forwards): 50–80
- Facebook groups and moms' clubs (6–8 posts at 10–20 each): 80–120
- Meta ads ($10/day for 3 weeks ≈ $210 at $3–4): 50–70
- Nextdoor, Instagram, partners, flyers: 20–40

If week 1 brings fewer than 40, the network and group posts aren't landing; fix the message before adding channels.

## Channel bets, ranked

Ranked by expected subscribers per hour of the owner's time, then per dollar. Everything is **assumed** until the experiment board says otherwise.

1. **The owner's network** (est. 50+ per hour). People who already trust her. Personal texts, not a broadcast. Ask each person to forward it to one friend. Do it once, early, and then mention it naturally.
2. **Local Facebook groups and moms' clubs** (est. 10–20 per post, ~10 min each). The biggest pool of warm local parents. Value first (3–4 real events), say she runs it, follow every group's rules, at most once every 2–3 weeks per group. Spring Valley Moms Club (240+ families), Spring Shadows Moms Club and Memorial Villages Moms come first.
3. **Meta ads** (est. $3–5 per subscriber, almost no owner time after setup). The only channel that scales with money. Leads objective with the Pixel, ZIPs 77024/77055/77080/77043/77079/77041. Start with three concepts and kill the losers after 5–7 days.
4. **Seasonal SEO guides + Facebook shares** (zero owner time for the guide, ~5 min to share). Thanksgiving break and winter break guides give her something genuinely useful to post in groups, which beats "please subscribe".
5. **Nextdoor** (est. 5–15 from one neighbor post). One post as herself, then a free business page. Low effort, but Nextdoor frowns on repeats.
6. **Partners** (slow to start, then they compound). Spring Branch Management District and Memorial Management District both have newsletters and social accounts; libraries and preschools have bulletin boards and parent emails. One or two emails a week, always offering something (we list their events, shout them out, give them a flyer).
7. **Instagram** (slow organically). Link in bio, the weekly carousel, one 10-second Reel a week, Collab posts with local kids' businesses. Keep it for trust and Collabs; don't expect it to drive the first 250.
8. **Referral** ("forward this to a friend" in every issue now). Ask Claude for a rewards program at ~300 subscribers.
9. **Giveaway with a local business** (try in week 4–5 once we have a partner). Entering means subscribing; follow Instagram's rules.
10. **Print flyers** with QR codes at the three libraries, indoor play spots and coffee shops. Cheap, slow, good for trust. Week 3+.
11. **Local media** (The Buzz Memorial edition, Community Impact). Pitch "a Spring Branch mom built a weekly guide for local families" in mid-November with a holiday angle, once there's something to point to.
12. **In person** (a library or community event table). Spring only; skip until the list is 500+.

## Budget

- **Ads:** $10/day from launch (Pixel first; until then Traffic → landing page views, or a $5–10/day Instagram boost). Three ads, equal split.
- **The rule:** after 5–7 days, turn off any ad above $5 per subscriber, move its money to the cheapest, and test one new concept. If the best ad is under $3 for two straight weeks, raise to $20/day.
- **Never** spend more to rescue a bad week. Fix the creative or the page first.
- Other money: flyers (~$20 printing), a giveaway prize only if a partner provides it.

## What would change this plan

- Join-page conversion under 10% from warm traffic → stop adding traffic and request a landing-page fix.
- Group posts averaging under 3 signups → change groups, timing or wording before posting more.
- Opens under 35% → work on subject lines before growth.
