# Littles City Launch Playbook

For a team opening the next Littles city (Atlanta, Chicago, Denver…) with no context. Built from the Spring Branch pilot, launched Oct 26, 2026.
**v0.1 (2026-10-25):** almost everything below is **assumed (not yet tested)**. Each Tuesday, results from Spring Branch replace assumptions with **proven (our data)**.

---

## 1. Pre-launch checklist (2–3 weeks before the first issue)

- [ ] **Pick the area tight.** One recognizable cluster of neighborhoods (Spring Branch & Memorial ≈ 6 ZIP codes), not the whole metro. Parents care about 15 minutes from home. Bigger cities get several Littles brands, not one big one.
- [ ] **Domain + handles:** `<area>littles.com`, Instagram, Facebook Page, Nextdoor business page. Same name everywhere.
- [ ] **Seed the listings before launch:** at least 10–15 dated local events in the next 3 weeks, 5+ weekly programs (library storytimes first), 12+ places (parks, libraries, free indoor play), 8+ kid-friendly restaurants and kids-eat-free deals. Spring Branch launched with 13 events, 7 weekly programs, 14 places, 12 restaurants, 7 deals.
- [ ] **School calendar:** the main district's breaks for the year (they drive the whole marketing calendar).
- [ ] **Newsletter:** Buttondown with the thank-you page set to `/thanks/` (that's how the Pixel counts signups); a "forward to a friend" line in every issue.
- [ ] **Meta:** Business portfolio, Page linked to Instagram, ad account, Pixel on the site firing "Lead" on `/thanks/`. Do this before any outreach so every signup is counted.
- [ ] **Tracking:** every link carries `?source=<tag>`; metrics collected weekly.
- [ ] **One seasonal guide published** before launch (the next school break).
- [ ] **Targets list:** 30+ local groups, schools, libraries, preschools, churches, kids' businesses and media, with contact routes (see §5).
- [ ] **Ad creative:** 3–4 concepts in feed/story/square sizes (Spring Branch: plan-ahead, storytime, weekend, holidays).

## 2. Week-by-week launch sequence (first 12 weeks)

Owner time: 30 min/day for 90 days. Money: $10–20/day ads.

## 4. Templates that worked

Not proven yet. Spring Branch launch-week templates are in `reach/kits/2026-10-25.md`: personal text (§1), Facebook "this week's picks" post (§2), "plan ahead" post (§3), Nextdoor (§4), district partner email (§7). Their numbers go here after week 1.

Patterns we're betting on:
- **Value first:** 3–4 real events with dates *before* any mention of signing up.
- **Disclose:** "I'm a local mom and I put this together", which is honest and is also the hook.
- **One link, one tag** per post.

## 5. What the next city needs (local research first)

1. **The school district calendar** (breaks drive the marketing calendar).
2. **Library systems:** which branches, their storytime calendars, their community boards. Often two systems (Spring Branch has county HCPL and city HPL).
3. **Moms' clubs and Facebook groups:** search Facebook for "<neighborhood> moms", "<city> moms of toddlers", "<district> parents". Many aren't indexed by Google; the owner has to search inside Facebook. Note rules and membership limits (Spring Valley Moms Club is residents-only).
4. **Management/civic districts and city newsletters:** Spring Branch has three districts (Spring Branch, Memorial, Energy Corridor) with newsletters and socials. Most US metros have equivalents (business improvement districts, civic associations).
5. **Preschools, MDOs and church children's ministries:** large church weekday schools have hundreds of families each.
6. **Kids' businesses** for Collabs and giveaways: gyms, classes, play spaces, kid-friendly restaurants.
7. **Local media:** neighborhood magazines and community papers.

For a bigger metro: split into several Littles brands by area from day one, and expect higher ad costs per subscriber.

## 6. Pitfalls

- (Watch) Launching outreach before the Pixel and thank-you page are set means the first and warmest signups aren't counted by Meta.
- (Watch) Metrics not connected at launch: we can't score channels without subscriber counts by source. Connect them before day 1 next time.
